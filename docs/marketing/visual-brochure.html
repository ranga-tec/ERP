The problem it solves

Manual files, paper approvals, disconnected department records, and delayed reports make it hard to know the real position of stock, service jobs, invoices, payments, and costs.

  • Excel-based stock and transaction tracking causes duplicate work and errors.
  • Purchase, stores, sales, service, and accounts teams often work from separate records.
