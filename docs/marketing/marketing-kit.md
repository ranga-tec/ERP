## Positioning

### Short Pitch

neuedge is a custom-built business management system for equipment, spare parts, service, trading, and distribution companies. It connects procurement, inventory, sales, service jobs, finance, reporting, audit logs, and user permissions in one web-based platform.

### One-Line Taglines

- Run procurement, inventory, sales, service, and finance from one ERP system.
- Replace spreadsheets with a complete ERP built for daily operations.
- Real-time control for equipment, spare parts, service jobs, and stock.
- One platform for purchasing, stores, sales, service, accounts, and management.
- Track every item, job, invoice, payment, and approval in one place.

### Target Customers

- Equipment distributors
- Spare parts dealers
- Vehicle and machinery service companies
- Trading and inventory-based businesses
- Multi-warehouse businesses
- Companies using Excel, paper documents, or disconnected software

### Main Pain Points

- Too much manual work in Excel
- Delayed stock visibility
- Difficult serial and batch tracking
- Slow service job follow-up
- Manual purchase order, GRN, invoice, and payment tracking
- Weak coordination between stores, sales, service, and accounts
- No clear audit trail for approvals and edits
- Time-consuming reports for owners and managers

### Core Benefits

- Real-time stock, sales, service, and finance visibility
- Faster document processing from request to invoice
- Better control over purchase orders, GRNs, dispatches, returns, and payments
- Stronger service job tracking with estimates, work orders, quality checks, handovers, and costing
- Role-based access for different departments
- Audit logs for accountability
- Management dashboards and reports for faster decisions

### Section: Built For Daily Operations

neuedge is designed around real business workflows. Users can create purchase requests, issue purchase orders, receive goods, manage stock, prepare quotes, dispatch items, raise invoices, track service jobs, settle payments, and view reports from one platform.

### Section: Connected Departments

- Procurement teams manage RFQs, purchase orders, GRNs, direct purchases, supplier invoices, and supplier returns.
- Stores teams manage stock availability, batch/serial tracking, warehouse/bin locations, adjustments, transfers, and reorder alerts.
- Sales teams manage quotations, sales orders, dispatches, invoices, and customer returns.
- Service teams manage equipment, contracts, service jobs, estimates, work orders, material requests, QC, expenses, and handovers.
- Finance teams manage AR/AP, payments, allocations, petty cash, credit notes, debit notes, and settlements.
- Management teams view dashboards, audit logs, reports, KPIs, and costing.

### Section: Technology

neuedge is built with modern web technologies:

- ASP.NET Core .NET 8 backend
- PostgreSQL database
- Next.js, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS frontend
- JWT authentication
- Role-based access
- Docker/VPS deployment support
