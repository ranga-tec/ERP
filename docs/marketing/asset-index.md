# neuedge Marketing Asset Index

This folder contains the reusable marketing pack for neuedge.

## Ready-To-Open Visual Files

- `visual-brochure.html` - full visual brochure with generated visuals, feature sections, and real product screenshots.
- `social-media-visual-pack.html` - square, landscape, and story-style social media visuals.
- `email-campaign-visual.html` - visual email template with hero image, module summary, screenshot, and CTA.
- `print-brochure.html` - simpler print-style brochure.

Open the HTML files in a browser. Use browser print/save-as-PDF for brochures and email previews. Use browser screenshots or design-tool import for social creatives.

## Written Copy Files

- `marketing-kit.md` - complete reusable marketing copy.
- `brochure-copy.md` - brochure-only copy.
- `email-whatsapp-templates.md` - email and WhatsApp templates.
- `social-media-posts.md` - LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, X, carousel, and paid ad copy.

## Feature Highlights To Use In Designs

- Master data: items, brands, categories, customers, suppliers, warehouses, bins, UoM, taxes, currencies, payment types, and references.
- Procurement: purchase requisitions, RFQs, purchase orders, goods receipts, direct purchases, supplier invoices, and returns.
- Inventory: on-hand stock, warehouse/bin visibility, stock adjustments, transfers, reorder alerts, batch/serial tracking, and stock ledger.
- Sales: quotations, sales orders, dispatches, direct dispatches, invoices, and customer returns.
- Service: equipment, contracts, service/repair jobs, estimates, revisions, work orders, material requests, quality checks, handovers, expense claims, and job costing.
- Finance: AR/AP, payments, allocations, petty cash, credit notes, debit notes, expense settlements, and approvals.
- Reporting: dashboard, stock ledger, aging, tax summary, service KPIs, costing, and audit logs.
- Security: role-based access, user permissions, approval control, and audit trails.

## Image Generation Prompts Used

These prompts can be reused in any image generation tool when you want raster versions.

### ERP Operations Hero

Use case: ads-marketing  
Asset type: ERP marketing hero image for brochure and social media  
Primary request: Create a realistic modern business operations scene for an ERP system used by equipment, spare parts, inventory, and service companies.  
Scene/backdrop: professional office and warehouse operations environment with subtle shelves, equipment parts, service desk, and finance/operations dashboard screens visible.  
Subject: diverse business operations team reviewing a large dashboard with procurement, inventory, sales, service, and finance indicators represented visually, no readable UI text.  
Style/medium: premium photorealistic commercial marketing image.  
Composition/framing: wide landscape composition, strong usable negative space on the left for brochure headline, dashboard/team on the right, balanced and clean.  
Lighting/mood: bright confident daylight, modern professional atmosphere.  
Color palette: white, charcoal, blue, green, and warm neutral accents.  
Constraints: no logos, no brand names, no watermark, no readable text, no distorted hands, no fake company names.

### Social Square Visual

Use case: ads-marketing  
Asset type: square social media post background for ERP software  
Primary request: Create a clean premium visual showing a laptop/tablet with an ERP dashboard concept, surrounded by subtle inventory, purchase order, invoice, service job, and finance workflow elements.  
Scene/backdrop: modern desk in a business office with hints of warehouse shelves in the background.  
Subject: laptop and tablet screens showing abstract dashboard cards, charts, workflow panels, and stock/service indicators; no readable text.  
Style/medium: photorealistic product-style business technology marketing image.  
Composition/framing: square composition, central device, enough top area for headline overlay, neat and uncluttered.  
Lighting/mood: bright, trustworthy, professional.  
Color palette: white, charcoal, blue, green, and small amber highlights.  
Constraints: no logos, no brand names, no watermark, no readable text, no misspelled interface labels.

### Service And Inventory Visual

Use case: ads-marketing  
Asset type: service and inventory ERP feature visual  
Primary request: Create a professional marketing image for ERP service management and inventory control.  
Scene/backdrop: clean industrial service workshop connected to a spare parts warehouse.  
Subject: service technician checking machinery parts with a tablet while shelves of spare parts, barcode labels, and a supervisor dashboard screen are visible in the background; no readable text.  
Style/medium: premium photorealistic commercial image.  
Composition/framing: landscape composition, technician and tablet in foreground, spare parts and workflow dashboard in background, clear space on upper left for headline overlay.  
Lighting/mood: bright, practical, trustworthy, operational.  
Color palette: white, blue, green, grey, and subtle yellow safety accents.  
Constraints: no logos, no brand names, no watermark, no readable text, avoid messy workshop clutter.
