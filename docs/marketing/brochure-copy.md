# neuedge Brochure Copy

## Front Cover

### Complete ERP and Service Management System

A web-based ERP platform for companies that manage procurement, inventory, sales, service jobs, finance, reporting, and user access control.

Take control of daily operations with one connected system.

## Inside Page 1: Why Businesses Need neuedge

Many businesses still manage critical operations through Excel sheets, paper documents, manual approvals, and disconnected software. As the business grows, this creates delays, duplicate work, weak stock visibility, slow service follow-up, and reporting gaps.

neuedge brings daily operations into one platform so each department can work with accurate records, controlled workflows, and real-time visibility.

## Inside Page 2: Core Modules

### Master Data

Items, brands, categories, suppliers, customers, warehouses, bins, UoM, taxes, currencies, payment types, and reference forms.

### Procurement

Purchase requisitions, RFQs, purchase orders, goods receipts, direct purchases, supplier invoices, and supplier returns.

### Inventory

On-hand stock, warehouse/bin tracking, stock adjustments, stock transfers, reorder alerts, batch and serial tracking, and stock ledger.

### Sales

Quotations, sales orders, dispatches, direct dispatches, invoices, customer returns, and payment visibility.

### Service Management

Equipment units, service contracts, service jobs, repair jobs, estimates, revisions, work orders, material requisitions, quality checks, handovers, expense claims, and job costing.

### Finance

AR/AP, payments, allocations, petty cash, credit notes, debit notes, expense settlements, and finance approvals.

### Reporting

Dashboard, stock ledger, aging, tax summary, service KPIs, costing, and management reports.

### Security

Role-based access, approval control, document traceability, and audit logs.

## Inside Page 3: Business Benefits

- Reduce spreadsheet dependency and repeated manual entry
- Improve stock accuracy and warehouse visibility
- Track purchase, sales, service, finance, and reporting workflows in one system
- Improve accountability through permissions and audit trails
- Give management clearer operational and financial insight
- Support multi-department coordination from request to invoice and payment
