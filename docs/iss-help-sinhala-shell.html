7.1 Accounts Receivable (AR)

Finance → Accounts Receivable — Customer invoice balances, outstanding. Posted sales invoices; payments reduce outstanding; credit notes allocate කළ විට reduce.

7.2 Accounts Payable (AP)

Finance → Accounts Payable — Supplier invoice/GRN balances. Posted supplier invoices; supplier payments reduce; credit notes reduce.
